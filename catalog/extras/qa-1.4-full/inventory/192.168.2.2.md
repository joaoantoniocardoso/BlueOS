## 15. Strand-risk (USB management @ 192.168.2.2)

Mutating these would drop host reachability on `192.168.2.0/24`:

| Risk | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **eth0** | Carries `192.168.2.2/24` and `default via 192.168.2.1 dev eth0` — **primary strand risk** |
| **default route on eth0** | Removing or moving default route off eth0 strands USB-tether host |
| **usb0** | Secondary gadget iface (`192.168.3.1`); reconfig/DHCP conflict could disrupt tether if bridged or swapped |
| **wlan0** | Not management today; enabling WiFi-only routing without eth0 fallback could strand |

**Safe to probe read-only:** wlan0 scan/status, ping sensors, kraken list, MCM streams (localhost).
